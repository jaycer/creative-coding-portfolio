Two subsets of the same face, taken verbatim from fonts.gstatic.com:
  anton-latin.woff2      U+0000-00FF and friends
  anton-latin-ext.woff2  the Latin Extended block

Self-hosted rather than linked from the Google Fonts CDN because this is the
only font in here that is not already on the machine, and it is the one the
whole app is about: iOS has no Impact, so without Anton every meme made on a
phone would come out in Helvetica. A page that ships verbatim from /public
should not need a network round trip to look like itself.
