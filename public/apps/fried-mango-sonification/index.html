    What you are hearing

    Beat
    Four on the floor: a kick on every day, a clap on two and four. Not data, the floor under it.
    Days
    A tick each day, with more of them as the search goes on.
    Postings captured
    A bass note, louder the more postings came in that day.
    Jobs pursued
    Thin high blips, one per job picked out, up to four.
    Applications sent
    An arpeggio, one note per application. A busy day plays faster.
    Interviews
    A rising two-note call.
    Offers
    The fanfare, with an echo.
    Milestones
    A crash on each captioned moment.

    Every pitched track plays notes from the same four-bar chord loop, so the layers stay in tune with each other. Nothing is random: one search always makes the same piece. At 2 days per second the tempo is 120 BPM, at 3 it is 180. Length is the whole piece: the days, then an outro and a last chord to cover a film that holds on its final frame.

    The exports

    MIDI
    One named track per layer, the tempo, and the captions as markers.
    DAW
    A DAWproject session for Bitwig, Studio One or Cubase: the same tracks with their colors and this mixer. Tracks arrive empty, so give each an instrument. Beat, Days and Milestones are drum notes, placed on the pads of a Bitwig Drum Machine kit.
    WAV
    The chip version, mixed as it stands, at exactly the length shown.

    The search file

    JSON. Days are counted from 0. Each job is five day numbers: found, applied, interview, offer, closed, with -1 for a stage it never reached. No names or employers are needed. 

    {
  "title": "A job search",
  "start": "2026-01-05",
  "days": 120,
  "captured": [12, 30, 0, 8],
  "jobs": [[0, 1, 14, -1, -1], [2, -1, -1, -1, -1]],
  "milestones": [[14, "The first interview."]]
}
