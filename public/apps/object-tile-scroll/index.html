Object Tile Scroll

Nothing that leaves ever comes back, and what arrives underneath was decided a moment ago.

How fast the field rises. At 1× a thing takes about half a minute to cross the frame.

Which way the field travels, as a bearing: 0 is up, 90 to the right, 180 falling. The lattice turns with it and objects are fed in from whichever edge is behind. Sound follows the picture, so a thing still pans to where it stands.

How far apart they stand. Wider is emptier and calmer; tighter fills the frame. The reading is roughly how many are in view at once.

The share that will not simply stand there: spinning flat, tumbling end over end, stuttering into a new attitude and staying wrong, breathing in and out, or drifting through colors. The rest ride up perfectly still, which is what makes the others read.

Sound

Every object plays its own voice, panned to where it stands and loudest through the middle of the frame. It fades as the thing leaves and is silent once the last of it is gone. Level is the slider in the header, and it starts at nothing.

The set

70 items: from a ladderback to a bathtub to a cheeseburger.
