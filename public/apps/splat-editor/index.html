Splat Editor

As Object Tile Scroll would show it

Open a scan

A .spz or a .ply from Scaniverse, Polycam or any other splat capture. Prefer the SPZ: it is the same points at a tenth the size. Nothing is uploaded, it stays in the page.

A .glb from splat2glb --trace-glb holds a cloud and the outlines traced round it in one file, so it opens on its own. A traces.json from --trace-out is the outlines without the cloud, and goes on top of one already open.
